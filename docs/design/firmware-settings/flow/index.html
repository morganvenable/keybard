Firmware update flow

Round 2 · Keybard installs firmware · every screen and its copy · IDs match update-flow.md · examples assume USB on the right half
Round 2. One sheet holds every choice, including what happens to the layout. After Start update Keybard asks nothing more, except one chord on the keyboard, held once at the start; it approves both halves. Every install is backup, install, put back. One list covers releases and a chosen file. The light guidance follows the firmware exactly (update-technical.md 1.10). Round 1's channel toggle (D3), restore prompt (A1) and backup-failed dialog (F15) are gone.

Updater colors show at about 38% brightness, even with lighting off or the board idle. Blue blinks once per update, at the start. Words appear only for magenta (both halves, "don't unplug") and the expected red between halves. Magenta is also layer 15's default color, and red is layers 6 and 7's, so the dialog is the authority, not the light.
